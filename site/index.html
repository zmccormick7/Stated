Locked in text

Systematic strategies run on numbers. But what moves a stock is often written down: a shift in strategy, a new risk, a promise quietly dropped. Sentiment scores and keyword counts capture how that text sounds, not what it says. Understanding it still takes an analyst, reading one company at a time.

If you can write it down, you can measure it.

Write a feature the way you'd brief an analyst.

LLMs can finally read like analysts.

Agents read whatever it takes, from one earnings call to everything a company's suppliers and customers have published.

Describe, don't predict.

LLMs know how history turned out. Ask one to predict a bank's prospects in March 2023 and it may simply remember what happened next. That lookahead bias looks like alpha in a backtest and disappears in live trading.

So our agents never predict anything. They describe a company as it stood on a date: guidance withdrawn, prices raised, a disclosure dropped. The forecasting is left to your models. And because knowing the ending doesn't help describe the starting point, every description is point-in-time by construction.

Across every company, every day.

Define the signals you care about, and Implied measures them across your entire universe, turning the written record into data your models can use.

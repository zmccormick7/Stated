Locked in text

Systematic strategies run on numbers. But what moves a stock is often written down: a shift in strategy, a new risk, a promise quietly dropped. Sentiment scores and keyword counts capture how that text sounds, not what it says. Understanding it still takes an analyst, reading one company at a time.

Now readable at scale

LLMs can finally read like analysts, across every company, every day.

Define the signals you care about, and Implied measures them across your entire universe, turning the written record into data your models can use.

Research that compounds

Deep research doesn't scale when every agent starts from scratch.

In Implied, agents share a knowledge graph, where each finding is recorded with its source, linked to related work, and revised as new evidence arrives. Many agents can work in parallel, and each one builds on everything the others have already learned.

Built for backtests

LLMs know how history turned out.

Ask one about a company in 2021 and it may remember what happened instead of reasoning about it. That looks like alpha in a backtest and disappears in live trading.

Our answer is to describe, not predict.

Each signal records what a company looked like on a given date, using only what was public then, and your own models do the forecasting. Knowing the ending doesn't help an agent describe the starting point, so every signal stays point-in-time.
